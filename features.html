Economy

Grind, farm and trade your way to the top of the money leaderboard.

  • Money to spend in the shop, and shards for the shard shop and crate shop.
  • Sell system with sell multipliers, so the more you grind the more you earn. Check what anything is worth first.
  • Farming meta: see which crops pay best right now.
  • Auction house to sell items to other players, and buy orders to request what you need.
  • Billford, a special trader with his own deals.
Commands
  • /shop
  • /shardshop
  • /crateshop
  • /sell
  • /sellall
  • /sellhand
  • /sellmulti
  • /worth
  • /meta
  • /ah
  • /orders
  • /bal
  • /pay <player> <amount>
  • /billford

PvP

Prove you're the best. Challenge a friend, queue up for a random opponent, or jump into the chaos.

  • Duels against a player of your choice, or join the duel queue.
  • FFA (free for all) with its own stats.
  • A ranked PvP arena.
  • Bounties on other players' heads.
  • Fast crystals for crystal PvP.
Commands
  • /duel <player>
  • /duel queue
  • /ffa
  • /ffastats
  • /pvp
  • /bounty
  • /leave

AFK zone & shards

Stand in the AFK zone to earn shards on a timer. Leaving the zone resets the timer, so stay put!

Shards are the purple second currency. Spend them in the shard shop and the crate shop.

Commands
  • /afk
  • /shards
  • /shardshop

Teams & friends

Team up, add friends and message each other. Somebody annoying? Ignore them.

Commands
  • /team
  • /friends
  • /msg <player>
  • /r
  • /ignore <player>

Getting around

Homes, warps, teleport requests and random teleport to get you into the wild fast, plus an RTP queue.

Commands

  • /rtp
  • /rtpq
  • /home
  • /sethome
  • /homes
  • /warp
  • /tpa <player>
  • /tpahere <player>
  • /tpaccept

Spawners

Stackable, Donut-style spawners. Stack them up to build a bigger, better farm in less space.

Tools & quality of life

  • Amethyst tools and vein mining.
  • A starter kit for new players.
  • A 6-row ender chest with /ec.
  • Night vision with /nv.
  • Triple jump at spawn and in the AFK world.
  • Leaderboards, stats and personal settings.
Commands
  • /ec
  • /nv
  • /lb
  • /stats
  • /settings

Proximity voice chat

Talk to nearby players with the Simple Voice Chat mod. The closer you are, the louder they sound. You get a voice chat prompt when you join.
