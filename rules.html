House rules

Keep it fair and fun for everyone. You can also read the rules in game with /rules.

⚠ Placeholder rules – the owner will replace these with the official server rules.

  1. No cheating or hacked clients [placeholder]

    No hacked clients, x-ray, auto-clickers or other unfair mods. Play with your own skill.

  2. No exploiting bugs [placeholder]

    Found a bug or a dupe? Report it to staff instead of using it.

  3. Be respectful in chat [placeholder]

    Trash talk in PvP is fine, but no harassment, hate speech, threats or bullying.

  4. No spamming or advertising [placeholder]

    Don't flood the chat, and don't advertise other servers or links.

  5. No inappropriate names or skins [placeholder]

    Keep usernames, skins, team names and builds suitable for everyone.

  6. Staff decisions are final [placeholder]

    Listen to staff. If you disagree with a decision, talk about it calmly on Discord.
