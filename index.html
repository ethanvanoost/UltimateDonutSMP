Hungry yet?

Join in 3 steps

  1. Open Minecraft

    Java Edition 26.2 (older versions work too) or Bedrock Edition.

  2. Add the server

    Multiplayer → Add Server, and paste ultimatedonutsmp.minekeep.gg

  3. Join & grab your kit

    Click Join Server, collect your starter kit and type /rtp to head into the wild.
