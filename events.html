Events

Something's always dropping

Supply drops to fight over, a daily advent calendar in December, and free keys every hour.

Supply drops

Crates from the sky

Every 20–45 minutes, a wave of crates lands in the supply drop world. Get there first and fight for the loot.

  • A wave of 2 to 10 crates lands every 20–45 minutes.
  • Type /event to enter, then follow the beacon beam to a crate.
  • Each wave lasts 15 minutes. Fight other players for the loot.
  • Loot can include money vouchers.
Next wave: 20–45 min
Advent calendar

24 days of rewards

Every day from December 1 to 24, claim a reward with /advent.

  • Missed a day? You can still claim it later.
  • Rare rewards are announced to the whole server.
